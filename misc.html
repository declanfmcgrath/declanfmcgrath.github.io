Holonomy playdough

Drag to rotate, or fling it to leave it spinning. Drag the circled vertices to reshape the loop. In sculpt mode, drag the surface outward or inward to model it like clay.

Signed rotation on return: –
A tangent vector carried around the loop by parallel transport comes back rotated, by the curvature the loop encloses. However you sculpt the surface, the total curvature is fixed at 4π (Gauss–Bonnet), so you can only move it around.
